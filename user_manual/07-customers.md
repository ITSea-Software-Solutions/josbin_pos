# Chapter 7 — Customers

Attaching a customer to a sale is optional but recommended. It enables email receipts, tracks customer spending, and is required for loyalty programs.

---

## 6.1 Attaching a customer to a sale

You can add a customer before or after adding products, but always **before** completing payment.

1. In the cart panel, find the **Customer** field at the top (shows "Walk-in customer" by default).
2. Click it to open the customer search panel.

---

## 6.2 Searching for an existing customer

1. Click the customer search field.
2. Type the customer's **name** or **phone number**. Results appear as you type.
3. Click the correct customer from the list.
4. The customer name now appears in the cart panel. Their email address will be pre-filled on the receipt screen.

---

## 6.3 Adding a new customer on the fly

You do not need to leave the POS screen to add a new customer.

1. In the customer search panel, click **"Add new customer"** (Dutch: *"Nieuwe klant toevoegen"*).
2. A small form appears. Fill in:
   - **Name** (required) — customer's full name
   - **Phone** (optional but recommended for search later)
   - **Email** (optional — required for email receipts)
3. Click **Save** (Dutch: *"Opslaan"*).
4. The new customer is saved and automatically attached to the current sale.

> **Privacy note:** Customer personal data (name, phone, email) is encrypted in the database. It is protected under WBP-S (Suriname personal data protection law).

---

## 6.4 Removing a customer from the current sale

1. In the cart panel, click the customer name.
2. Click the **× (remove)** button next to their name.
3. The sale reverts to "Walk-in customer".

---

## 6.5 Default walk-in customer

Every sale without a named customer is recorded as a **walk-in** (anonymous customer). This is the default and is perfectly fine for most transactions.

Walk-in sales are included in all reports and totals. They are simply not linked to a specific person.

---

## 6.6 Customer purchase history

The customer's total spending and visit count are updated automatically after every sale they are attached to. Managers can view this in the admin dashboard.

---

## 6.7 GDPR / WBP-S note for staff

- Customer data (name, phone number, email) may only be collected with the customer's knowledge and consent.
- Do not enter personal data of customers who have not agreed to it.
- If a customer asks to have their data removed, contact your system administrator.
