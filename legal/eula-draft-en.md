## 1. Definitions

- **Licence Certificate:** Annex A, recording per installation: the
  organisation, number of stores, number of POS terminals, licence tier,
  start date, end date and fee.
- **Installation:** one store server with its connected POS terminals at
  one store address.
- **Licence Server:** the service operated by Licensor against which the
  Software verifies its licence status.
- **Customer Data:** all data entered into or generated by the Software by
  or for the Customer (sales, products, customer records, reports, audit
  log).

## 2. Grant of licence

2.1 Licensor grants the Customer a **non-exclusive, non-transferable
licence** to use the Software for its own business operations, limited to
the number of stores and POS terminals stated in the Licence Certificate.

2.2 The Software is **licensed, not sold**. All intellectual-property
rights remain with Licensor [and/or its software-development supplier].

2.3 Expansion (an additional store or terminal) requires an amended
Licence Certificate; the Software displays "licence limit reached" on
terminals beyond the licensed count until the expansion is recorded.

## 3. Restrictions

The Customer shall not, and shall not permit any third party to:

a. copy the Software other than for backup purposes within its own
   Installation;
b. rent out, sublicense, resell or otherwise make the Software available
   to third parties;
c. reverse-engineer, decompile or disassemble the Software, including any
   attempt to render the encrypted (IonCube-encoded) files readable,
   except to the extent mandatory law permits;
d. circumvent, disable or tamper with the licence enforcement (hardware
   binding, licence checks, locks);
e. remove trademark, ownership or copyright notices.

## 4. Licence enforcement and telemetry

4.1 Each Installation is bound to a hardware fingerprint (including
network address, processor ID and installation ID). Moving to different
hardware requires re-issuance by Licensor.

4.2 The Software contacts the Licence Server at start-up and at least
daily. Only licence data is transmitted (licence ID, fingerprint,
store/terminal counts, software version). **No sales or customer data is
transmitted.**

4.3 If the Licence Server is temporarily unreachable, the Software
continues to function unchanged for at least **72 hours** (offline grace).

## 5. Term, renewal and locking

5.1 The licence runs for the period in the Licence Certificate and is
renewed by timely payment of the renewal fee.

5.2 Around and after the end date, the following ladder applies, enforced
by the Software itself:

| Moment | Effect |
|---|---|
| 30 days before end date | Full operation; dashboard + e-mail reminder |
| 14 days before end date | Full operation; daily reminders |
| End date → +14 days (grace) | **Full operation**; warning visible to managers only |
| +14 days (soft lock) | **New sales blocked.** All existing data, reports, BTW exports and audit exports remain fully accessible |
| Soft lock +30 days (hard lock) | Sign-in blocked; data export remains available on request for 90 days (art. 6) |
| Renewal (at any time) | Instant reactivation, no reinstallation |

## 6. Customer Data — ownership and continuity

6.1 **Customer Data is and remains the property of the Customer.**

6.2 Even under soft or hard lock, the Customer retains the right to export
Customer Data in common formats (CSV/PDF; database copy on request). Under
hard lock this export facility remains available for at least **90 days**.
Licensor never holds Customer Data "hostage" beyond the functional locking
described in art. 5.

6.3 Upon termination, Licensor deletes copies of Customer Data in its
possession at the Customer's written request, subject to statutory
retention obligations.

## 8. Privacy (WBP-S)

8.1 For personal data in the Software, the Customer is the controller;
where Licensor processes personal data in the course of support or
administration, it acts as processor.

8.2 Where required — in any case for government engagements — the Parties
conclude a separate **Data Processing Agreement (Verwerkersovereenkomst)**
under the Suriname Personal Data Protection Act (WBP-S). [Annex B.]

## 9. Audit

9.1 Licensor may verify compliance with the licensed counts
(stores/terminals) through the licence telemetry of art. 4.

9.2 In case of reasonable doubt, Licensor may verify compliance on site
with at least [10] business days' notice, during office hours, without
unnecessary disruption of operations.

## 10. Warranty and liability

10.1 Licensor undertakes that the Software materially functions as
described in the documentation and will repair reported defects within a
reasonable period. Otherwise the Software is provided "as is".

10.2 Licensor's total liability per year is limited to the licence fees
paid by the Customer in the preceding 12 months, except in cases of intent
or deliberate recklessness. [Have the liability regime reviewed against
Surinamese law.]

10.3 Licensor is not liable for indirect damage, lost profit or
consequential damage, nor for damage caused by use contrary to the
documentation or by modifications not made by Licensor.

## 11. Termination

11.1 Either Party may terminate in writing upon a material breach not
remedied within [30] days of notice of default.

11.2 Upon termination the right of use ends; art. 6 (data export), art. 8
(privacy) and art. 10 (liability) survive.

## 12. Final provisions

12.1 This agreement is governed by the **law of Suriname**; disputes are
submitted to the competent court in **Paramaribo** [or arbitration — for
the lawyer/parties to decide].

12.2 Rights under this agreement are not assignable without written
consent. Invalidity of one clause leaves the remainder intact. This
agreement and its annexes constitute the entire agreement.
