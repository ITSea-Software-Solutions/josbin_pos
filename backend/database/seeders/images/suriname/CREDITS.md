# Product photo credits — Josbin POS demo catalogue

All images below are from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown. They are
demo data: a real store replaces them with its own shelf photos (product form
→ image upload, or the bulk import).

Six products deliberately have NO photo and fall back to the drawn category
glyph — dry beans, charcoal, taro leaf, flour, cooking oil and kwikwi. Commons
keyword search returned a 1943 gardening poster, an 1888 lumber-trade magazine
cover and a plate of sticky-rice parcels for those. A drawing that is honestly a
drawing beats a photograph of the wrong thing.
